# IPC-101 Rev 2 planning notes

Recorded 2026-09-25 from user direction. This is the next-board requirements backlog, not a released PCB revision or authorization to fabricate. Current KiCad/Rev C fabrication files are unchanged. "Rev 2" is the user's next-board designation; assign the formal fabrication revision consistently before release.

## System context

IPC-101 replaces the improvised Alpha panel now, connected to the existing ESP32; IPC-100 is still in development and is a later host. Build a new Alpha harness instead of assuming the old panel harness is directly compatible. The Crosswind repo contains the Alpha firmware and adapter plan: GPIO21 SDA, GPIO22 SCL, GPIO5 OLED reset, OLED and BME280 on the shared bus. IPC-101 keypad/RGB firmware migration and STOP integration remain separate unfinished work. Do not apply IPC-100-only interface assumptions to Alpha without reconciliation.

## Requested board changes

**Mounting direction confirmed 2026-09-26:** Rev 2 should carry the front-panel electronics on the PCB as one aligned assembly: ARM/PULL switches, navigation switch, RGB LED and OLED. Use component selection, accurate footprints and a coordinated PCB-to-faceplate height to avoid separate faceplate-mounted electronic components, flying leads, LED harness connectors, and individual printed retainers/brackets. The faceplate provides openings and access; establish component alignment and support from the PCB. Necessary OLED supports should attach the module to the PCB assembly. The LED pigtail/faceplate-retainer discussion applies only to salvaging the current board, not to the Rev 2 design.

Check the mounting stack with actual components before layout release: LED projection and support, OLED window position/height, navigation travel and ARM/PULL free play must work at the same PCB-to-faceplate spacing. Do not rely on long unsupported LED leads to establish height or carry panel loads. Preserve the existing roof-mounted STOP arrangement pending an explicit redesign; this front-panel integration requirement does not silently relocate the received STOP switch or change its circuit. Prefer PCB-mounted BME280 integration where ambient sensing can be preserved; document any need for remote placement before choosing it.

## Related physical feedback to carry forward

- LED fit, 2026-09-26: the user confirms the four holes are empty and the leads do not enter at all. Approximately 0.5 mm square leads would have a 0.707 mm diagonal, explaining interference with a 0.60 mm hole, but square leads are only a hypothesis. Actual finished-hole diameter and lead dimensions remain to be measured. Do not force leads or drill out plated holes. A prototype workaround is short individually insulated wire extensions that fit the existing holes, with independent mechanical LED support and verified R/anode/G/B mapping; this workaround has not been built or tested.
- ARM/PULL stems: user removed approximately 1.5 mm from PULL and reports good operation. User requests the same reduction for both caps; ARM is not yet physically verified. This is a cap/mechanical correction, not grounds to relocate switches without a complete stack check. Current nominal R1 dimensions imply 8.07 mm stem beyond the rear flange after that reduction.
- Roof R2 accepts the STOP block; R3 adds access to its push-up release. Keep STOP service access in the populated-board and harness assembly review.
- The rear tunnel's 50 mm bore ring passed the user's connector clearance check. Full harness bending and slide-and-pull service motion are not yet validated.
- Direct OLED mounting and rear connector changes can alter the PCB/faceplate/carrier stack. Preserve proven datums where possible and explicitly revise affected enclosure parts if necessary.

## Release checks for the next board

1. Record the actual BME module, 504 pin measurements, OLED connector/mount geometry, RGB LED maximum lead dimensions/pinout and rear harness mating envelope.
2. Establish a dimensioned component-side and rear-side assembly layout, including the populated R3/cradle/faceplate stack and accessible connector latches. Verify the directly PCB-mounted LED, OLED and switches together at the chosen panel spacing, including mounting support, tolerances and control travel; current-board pigtail/retainer workarounds are not the Rev 2 mounting solution.
3. Reconcile Alpha and future IPC-100 power, I2C topology, pull-ups, reset, connector pinouts and separate STOP handling. Document required firmware changes; do not assume hardware wiring alone enables IPC-101.
4. Print a 1:1 PCB layout or mechanical gauge and trial-fit the received 504, OLED and sensor hardware before ordering. Verify LED lead clearance with a suitable hole gauge or footprint coupon; a paper layout alone cannot establish plated-hole fit.
5. Complete schematic/PCB review, ERC/DRC, silkscreen/polarity inspection, and updated BOM, assembly drawings, harness documentation and fabrication revision marking.
6. Bench-check the assembled board, display, BME and each control before motion integration. Physical fit does not establish electrical or firmware acceptance.

## RGB LED candidate and purchasing limits

Candidate for the revised footprint: **Kingbright WP154A4SEJ3VBDZGW/CA**, a 5 mm diffused common-anode RGB indicator. This is not an approved drop-in replacement for Rev C's 0.60 mm holes. No researched alternative has been verified to fit those holes directly. For the existing board, retaining the received LED with insulated wire extensions avoids buying another mechanically incompatible part.

- [Manufacturer datasheet](https://www.kingbrightusa.com/images/catalog/SPEC/WP154A4SEJ3VBDZGW-CA.pdf), revision 7A, reviewed 2026-09-26. Use the full dimension tolerances, package flange and actual pinout when revising the footprint and panel support; do not assume the current R-A-G-B order matches a replacement.
- [DigiKey product listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/kingbright/WP154A4SEJ3VBDZGW-CA/6569334).
- Electrical qualification is required: the datasheet gives typical green/blue forward voltage of 3.3 V at 20 mA, so the current 3.3 V circuit cannot be assumed to supply that test current. Check actual indicator brightness at the intended lower current, resistor values, driver sink limits and voltage headroom before selecting this part for release. Do not raise the common-anode supply without reviewing the driver circuit.
- Freeze a manufacturer part number in the BOM and update the footprint/generator, assembly pinout and fabrication outputs together after qualification. Current PCB and fabrication files are unchanged by this planning update.
