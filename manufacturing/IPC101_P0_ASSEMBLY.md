# IPC-101 P0 Assembly and Inspection

1. Order one SW3 and U1 before PCB release. Overlay both on 1:1 footprint plots.
2. Stencil and reflow U1 first; inspect exposed-pad wetting by process coupon/X-ray if available.
3. Fit 0805 passives and J1, then verify 3V3-to-GND resistance and U1 pin-1 orientation.
4. Fit SW1/SW2/SW3 without mechanical preload. Keep flux/residue out of switch bodies.
5. Fit J3 but maintain its isolated-net clearance; do not connect either STOP net to fixture ground.
6. Install test loops and mechanically mount the OLED only after electrical inspection.
7. Clean only with processes approved for the selected switches/connectors. Do not wash unsealed tactiles by default.
8. Apply board serial/revision label and inspect all front/rear legends.

P0 is prototype workmanship, IPC-A-610 Class 2 target. Conformal coating, sealing, cap adhesive, and final torque remain blocked on enclosure/material selection.
