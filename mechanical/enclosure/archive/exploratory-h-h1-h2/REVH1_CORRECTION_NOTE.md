# Rev H1 correction note

Status: **TEST-FIT / NOT FROZEN**

Rev H was rejected after visual review because:
- the temporary corner stack bosses projected outside the enclosure as visible "nubs";
- the oversized service-opening subtraction removed too much of the lower shell, leaving the underside open.

Rev H1 corrects those issues:
- no external PCB/carrier bosses;
- closed continuous lower/underside shell;
- Rev G rear wood-mount hole pattern and mounting-pad concept retained;
- front opening is limited to the inclined faceplate service opening;
- current ~16.5 mm faceplate-back to PCB-top target remains provisional;
- PCB carrier/chassis will be a separate internal part so the enclosure exterior remains clean;
- a generic connector-cassette opening is reserved on the right rear side, not on the wood-contact mounting plane.

Rev H is superseded by Rev H1 for further mechanical review. Do not print Rev H.
